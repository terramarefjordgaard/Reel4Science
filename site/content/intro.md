Data hub of the ACCESS-Fjord project
===

**ACCESS-Fjord** is a Terramare project connecting recreational fishing, citizen science and marine research in Sørfolda, Northern Norway.

The visitors of the Terramare fish-camp are engaged in the scientific monitoring survey of the fjord, participating in fishing trips and recording catches.

The Reel4Science data hub gathers data as they are produced at sea. Choose a period, explore the stations, follow each species and meet the community that participated in the survey.
